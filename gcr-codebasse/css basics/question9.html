Technology and Modern Life

Technology has changed almost every part of modern life. People now use smartphones, computers, and the internet for communication, education, entertainment, and work. Digital technologies continue to develop rapidly and create new opportunities for individuals and businesses.

The growth of artificial intelligence has introduced new ways of solving complex problems. AI systems can analyze large amounts of information and help people make better decisions. However, responsible use of technology remains important for society.

Web development is also changing continuously. Developers use modern HTML, CSS, and programming languages to build responsive and accessible websites for users on different devices and screen sizes.
